## Visual Identity Principles

### 1. The Berry IS the Brand (Heritage Principle)
The berry is not decoration — it IS ColaberryAI's identity. The original colaberry.com logo embedded a berry in the letterforms. This MUST continue. Any logo that doesn't reference berry heritage is rejected.

**Applications:**
- "o" in Colaberry → berry shape (circle + micro-stem)
- Standalone mark → berry/node hybrid
- Berry cluster → knowledge graph visualization
- Berry color → accent (#DC2626 berry red)

### 2. Intelligence Lives in the Letters (Embedding Principle)
Visual clues for "AI" must be embedded IN the letterforms, not bolted on. The clue should be a natural part of the letter's anatomy.

**Letterform Anatomy for Clue Embedding:**
| Letter | Anatomy Parts | Embedding Opportunities |
|--------|--------------|----------------------|
| A | Apex, crossbar, legs, counter | Crossbar = brainwave/signal, counter = eye/neural |
| I | Stem, tittle (dot), serifs | Tittle = neural node/berry, stem = antenna/signal |
| a (lowercase) | Bowl, stem, terminal | Bowl = brain/berry, terminal = circuit |

**Techniques:**
- **Crossbar modification:** A's crossbar becomes a sine wave (intelligence signal)
- **Tittle replacement:** I's dot becomes a berry node or neural pulse
- **Counter shaping:** A's interior triangle becomes an eye or neural network
- **Ligature creation:** AI connected by a single flowing stroke (intelligence flowing)

### 3. Science Whispers, Not Shouts (Labs Principle)
"Research Labs" is a sub-text — its visual clue should be the most subtle. A micro-detail that only the most attentive viewer catches.

**Subtle Science Indicators:**
- A tiny atom orbit around a letter dot
- A DNA helix curve replacing a letter's tail
- A beaker silhouette in the negative space of a letter
- Hexagonal grid texture at micro-scale
- A lens/circle motif in the "a" of "Labs"

### 4. Restraint = Premium (Luxury Principle)
Enterprise logos communicate authority through what they REMOVE, not what they add. Every element must earn its place. If removing an element doesn't hurt the logo, remove it.

**Complexity Budget:**
| Component | Max SVG Elements | Rationale |
|-----------|-----------------|-----------|
| Standalone mark | 10 | Must work at 16px favicon |
| Wordmark modifications | 5 per modified letter | Don't over-engineer letterforms |
| Total logo system | 20 | Entire logo (mark + text + clues) |

### 5. Dark-First, Mode-Agnostic (Technical Principle)
The logo is designed for dark mode (#09090B) first, then adapted to light. It must be beautiful in both without requiring separate designs.

## Clue Target Specification

### The Clue Brief (For @canvas-designer)

Every concept MUST document clues using this template:

```markdown
### Clue Map for [Concept Name]

| Target | Element | Technique | Surface Read | Subconscious Read | Discovery Moment |
|--------|---------|-----------|-------------|-------------------|-----------------|
| Co/Berry | [letter/shape] | [modification type] | [what you see first] | [what you realize] | "[reaction quote]" |
| AI | [letter/shape] | [modification type] | [what you see first] | [what you realize] | "[reaction quote]" |
| Labs | [letter/shape] | [modification type] | [what you see first] | [what you realize] | "[reaction quote]" |
```

**Quality Criteria for Clues:**
| Quality | Description | Example |
|---------|------------|---------|
| ❌ Forced | Clue is obvious, feels gimmicky | Brain emoji replacing a letter |
| ❌ Invisible | Clue is too subtle, no one notices | 0.5px line difference |
| ✅ Subconscious | Clue registers without conscious effort | FedEx arrow — you feel "speed" before seeing it |
| ✅✅ Delightful | Clue creates genuine "aha!" moment | Amazon A-to-Z — "Oh! It's A to Z AND a smile!" |
